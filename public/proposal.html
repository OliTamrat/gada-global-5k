Executive Summary

Olink Technologies is pleased to present this proposal for the Gada Global 5K — an annual community running event celebrating Oromo heritage and the Irrecha thanksgiving festival. Scheduled for October 3, 2026 at Rock Creek Parkway in Washington, DC, this inaugural event will bring together up to 500 runners and community members.

This document outlines the complete digital platform built to power the event — from marketing and athlete registration to merchandise sales, live race results with AI-powered timing, and secure payment processing. The platform is designed as a reusable foundation for annual events going forward.

What We Are Delivering

A fully custom Next.js web application with athlete registration, 3-tier pricing, Stripe payment processing, e-commerce merch shop with cart, live race timing & results, dispute resolution system, animated hero with video background, live countdown, and premium sports-inspired design — ready to launch and accept registrations.

Platform Overview

Marketing Website

8-section landing page with video hero, animated word rotator, countdown, athlete gallery, and cultural content

Registration System

3-tier pricing (Early Bird $25, Standard $35, Race Week $45) with Stripe Checkout

Live Race Results & Timing

A complete race-day results system that provides real-time leaderboard tracking, individual runner pages, QR code scanning at the finish line, and a dispute resolution workflow — features typically only available through expensive third-party race timing providers.

Live Leaderboard

Real-time results page showing all finishers ranked by time, with search and category filters (Overall, Male, Female)

Individual Runner Pages

Each runner gets a /race/[bib] page showing their name, finish time, pace, placement, and ranking

QR Code Scanning

Finish-line scanning page for race officials to record finish times by entering bib numbers

Timing Disputes

Structured dispute submission and review workflow with admin resolution for timing corrections

Race Day Workflow
1. Pre-RaceRegistered runners appear on the leaderboard page with "Waiting" status
2. Finish LineRace official enters bib numbers at /race/scan as runners cross the finish
3. ResultsLeaderboard updates in real-time. Runners can search their bib or name
4. DisputesRunners can flag timing issues via /race/disputes with photo evidence upload
5. ResolutionAdmin reviews disputes, applies corrections or rejects with explanation

Revenue Potential

With 500 participants and an estimated 60% merch purchase rate, the store can generate $4,200 – $8,400+ in additional revenue beyond registration fees. T-shirts can also be sold to non-participants through the public shop page.
